LIVEWORK design pass, September 6 2026
======================================

Drag everything in this folder into the root of the LIVEWORK repo and
let it overwrite. Nothing here needs an SQL patch or a function deploy.

WHAT CHANGED

  The yellow          #E3BD3B is gone, #F2C632 everywhere
  The wordmark        your light cut above 120px, a heavy cut below it
  The app icon        black tile, yellow L|W, drawn as vector
  Clients             the word Companies is gone from the whole interface
  Light and dark      a sun and moon switch, top right on a phone, in the
                      rail on a desktop. It remembers your choice; with no
                      choice made your phone's own setting decides
  Status              draft grey, sent blue, paid green, past due red,
                      void as a ring so it does not read as another grey dot
  Tab icons           roof, building, head, columns, rules. No two of them
                      read the same at thumb size
  The fonts           see below, this one matters

THE FONTS

  Every Manrope file in the repo was the wrong weight:

    Manrope-ExtraBold   was actually SemiBold
    Manrope-SemiBold    was actually Medium
    Manrope-Regular     was actually Light

  So the site, the desk and every PDF have been rendering a full weight
  light since the first build. Correct files are in this folder under the
  same names. Everything will look heavier the moment you drag them.
