Fewer resumes. Better decisions.

Modern work brings together specialized expertise, transferable skills, and changing expectations about where and how work happens. LIVEWORK combines research, structured evaluation, and technology with human judgment to make those connections more thoughtfully.

Our approach considers the full context of a role: the skills required, the work arrangement, the team’s operating needs, and the candidate’s goals. Technology supports the process. People remain accountable for the judgment.

LIVEWORK is a talent and recruiting firm based in Orange County, California, built for the evolving demands of modern work. We combine informed human judgment with technology to understand complex skill sets, align professional ambitions with business needs, and develop more considered matches across on-site, remote, and hybrid environments. Our purpose is to improve the lives of the people we serve and help companies build the teams they need today to succeed tomorrow.
