# LIVEWORK desk manual

This is what the helper knows. Plain answers, one screen at a time. Edit this file to teach it more.

## The three surfaces
- The desk (livework.inc/desk.html) is staff only. Sign in with your LIVEWORK email and the six digit code that is emailed to you. No password.
- The client room (livework.inc/room.html) is for clients. A contact gets in only when a staff member turns Room on for them on the client's Contacts tab. They sign in the same way, email and a six digit code.
- The site (livework.inc) is the public marketing page. Companies and candidates write in from the contact form. Hire messages go to team@livework.inc, job seekers to careers@livework.inc.
- LIVESIGN (livework.inc/livesign.html) signs any PDF. Open it from the bottom of the rail, or More on the phone.

## Getting around
- Desktop: the black rail on the left lists every screen. Phone: five tabs across the bottom, Today, Clients, Roles, Candidates, More. More opens the rest.
- The sun and moon switch at the bottom of the rail flips light and dark.
- Every row opens the record. Most screens have a search box and chips to filter.
- Sign out is at the bottom of the rail under your name.

## Today
- Today is your home page. "What needs you" is one ranked list built from the data, nothing is typed into it: open roles sitting 3 or more days with nobody submitted, candidates stuck 7 or more days at a stage, interviews that happened with no write up, clients with an open role and no contact in 14 days, overdue tasks. Each row has one button that does the thing.
- Admins see Mine and Everyone chips to change scope. Recruiters see their own accounts.
- The + Add button top right adds a client, candidate, role, task or interview.
- Billing on Today (admin only) lists invoices that need a send.

## Clients
- Clients lists each client company with its key contact, the last time someone spoke to them, and Call, Email, Open. Call and Email log the activity by themselves, so you never type "called Dana".
- Chips: Open searches (clients with a live role) or Everyone. The search box matches company names and contact names.
- A red "No agreement" pill means the client has no signed agreement on file, so the account is incomplete. Draw one up from the Documents tab.
- Inside a client: Profile (legal name, notice address, notice email, terms), Contacts, Roles, Documents, Activity.
- Contacts tab: each person has Call, Email and two switches. Room lets them into the client room. Billing marks who invoices go to (one per client, needs a work email).

## Agreements and signing
- Documents tab on a client: "Draw up an agreement". Pick the kind: Client Recruiting Agreement (account wide, contingent, 20 percent), Retained Search Agreement (one role, 10,000 retainer credited against the fee), or Mutual NDA.
- The form only asks for what the record does not already know. Press Create it. Read the PDF in the preview, page through it, then either Approve and send for signature (the client gets an emailed link, opens it, draws a signature with a finger, and both of you get the executed copy with an audit page) or Email it for a wet signature (attaches the PDF to a mail). Change what prints and redo it reopens the inputs.
- A client with a signed Client Recruiting Agreement is complete. A retained role also needs its own signed Retained Search Agreement.
- Upload a signed copy is for agreements signed outside the desk.
- LIVESIGN signs any other PDF: open livesign.html, choose the PDF, it finds the signature, name and date fields on a fillable form or you tap the page to place them, drag to move, pull the yellow corner to resize, x to remove, sign once with your finger, Sign and send. The signed PDF with its audit page is emailed to the address you typed and to you. LIVEWORK keeps no copy of the document.

## Roles (searches)
- A role belongs to a client. Status is open, filled, cancelled or on hold. Retained roles carry the retainer.
- Open roles with nobody submitted for 3 days show on Today.
- Advertising for a role starts when you launch the search; the client gets a notice.

## Candidates
- Candidates is one shared pool for the whole team. Each row: name, status, title and location, last contact, then Call, Email, Open, and a follow up line under it. Sorted so overdue follow ups ride the top.
- Add one by hand, or Drop resumes to reach Intake.
- Premier marks a strong person to recruit later even if there is no role now. Signal shows recent movement.
- A candidate's Documents tab has the branded profile PDF and the client copy with no contact details.

## Intake (resumes)
- Drop up to ten PDF resumes. They land in the tray. Press Read N and they are read one at a time by the AI reader, which fills name, title, email, phone, location, summary and skills. Rough read means the AI was not available and only a basic scan ran.
- Check each card, pick the industry, press Save all. Duplicates by email update the existing candidate instead of making a second one. A branded profile PDF is made for each.
- Word files are not accepted yet, PDF only.

## Pipeline
- The board shows each role's candidates by stage: sourced, screened, submitted, client interview, offer, placed, passed. Advance moves a candidate forward. Release makes the candidate visible in the client room. Held candidates are yours only.
- When the client advances, passes or asks for a time in the room, the desk gets a task and hello@ gets an email.

## Interviews
- Add an interview from + Add on Today. It records when, what kind, and a going in note. There are no calendar invites or meeting links; use Outlook and Teams for that. After it happens Today asks you to write it up.

## Placements and invoices
- A placement records the hire: candidate, role, start date, base salary, fee.
- Raise an invoice from a placement and it fills the client, amount, description and due date (start date plus 30). Invoices start as Draft. Numbers are LW-1001 and up.
- Buttons on an invoice: PDF, Send, then Remind and Chase as it ages, Paid. Nothing sends by itself, every send is a press. Reminders go 7 days before due, on the due day, then at 7, 15 and 30 days past due. Final notice at 30.
- Invoices go to the client's Billing contact, else the first contact with an email, else the AP email on the client.
- Remit details never change by email. If a client asks, that is a fraud attempt.

## Mail and activity
- Mail composes branded emails from the desk, logged to the client or candidate. Activity is the log of every call, email, note, visit and stage change, with opens and clicks on sends.

## People (admin only)
- People manages staff: name, email, role (admin, recruiter, coordinator), active. Only admins see it.

## House rules
- Log every touch, or let Call and Email log it for you.
- Never send a candidate to a client without releasing them on the pipeline.
- Never invoice without a placement.
- One billing contact per client.
- No agreement, no search.

## Words people use
- "Scan a document" or "upload a document": there is no scanner in the desk. Make a PDF (a phone photo saved as PDF works) and upload it. A signed agreement goes on the client's Documents tab, Upload a signed copy. A resume goes to Intake, Drop resumes. A document that still needs signing goes to LIVESIGN.
- "Attach a file to a candidate": the candidate's Documents tab.
- "Send a contract" or "get a signature": the client's Documents tab, Draw up an agreement, then Approve and send for signature. Any other PDF, use LIVESIGN.
- "Add someone to the portal" or "give the client access": the client's Contacts tab, turn Room on for that person.
- "Log a call": press Call on the row, it logs itself. Or Activity, Add.
- "Bill the client": Placements first, then Raise an invoice from the placement.
