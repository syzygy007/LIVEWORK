Our relationship

These Candidate Terms govern recruiting services provided to you by GERMANN INC, a California corporation doing business as LIVEWORK (“LIVEWORK,” “we,” or “us”), when you affirmatively accept them or otherwise expressly agree to participate on these terms. The Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information. The Terms of Use apply to use of the Site when accepted.

We seek to understand your experience, capabilities, goals, and preferred way of working so we can consider opportunities aligned with both your professional ambitions and the requirements of a role. Our purpose is to help you make informed career decisions; participation does not guarantee an interview, offer, or placement.

No candidate fees

LIVEWORK’s recruiting fees are paid by employer clients. We do not charge candidates a recruiting fee, deposit, subscription, or percentage of compensation. A client’s fee obligation is not your obligation and may not be passed to you under these Terms.

If anyone claiming to represent LIVEWORK requests payment for access to a role or placement, contact legal@livework.inc before taking action.

Your information and participation

Provide information that is accurate to the best of your knowledge and promptly correct material inaccuracies. Tell us about relevant changes in availability, qualifications, or compensation expectations. Please notify us if you are already in an active process for a role before we arrange an introduction.

We ask that you attend interviews you accept, communicate scheduling changes, and tell us when you withdraw or accept an offer. Do not provide confidential material belonging to an employer or another person. We do not request salary history; you may discuss your expectations for a future role.

Authorization to present your profile

We will obtain your affirmative authorization before sending an identifiable profile to a specified company for a specified opportunity. We will identify the company and role and describe any material confidentiality restrictions we know. If a search requires the client’s identity to remain confidential initially, we may discuss the opportunity or use an appropriately de-identified summary, but we will not release your identifiable profile before obtaining the required authorization.

Any authorization covering multiple companies must identify the permitted recipients or an objectively defined scope, the purpose, and its duration. A general inquiry or resume submission does not authorize unrestricted distribution. You may decline an opportunity or withdraw authorization for future disclosures at any time.

Your resume and technology

You retain rights in your resume and other materials. You authorize us to organize and format them for approved recruiting purposes. We may apply LIVEWORK branding and a reference identifier without changing the underlying facts. You may request a copy of the profile presented on your behalf and ask us to correct it.

We may use software and AI-assisted tools to extract resume information, identify skills, and support research and matching. Our recruiting process requires meaningful human review of profiles and recommendations before presentation. We do not use solely automated decisions to determine whether to present you for a role. Technology can make mistakes; contact careers@livework.inc to request correction or human consideration of relevant information.

A client may use its own recruiting technologies under its own notices and responsibilities. Where a particular use requires additional notice, consent, or an alternative process, the responsible party must provide it before that use.

Equal opportunity and access

We evaluate job-related qualifications, experience, transferable skills, and stated work preferences. We do not accept discriminatory client instructions or use protected characteristics as a substitute for legitimate role requirements. Applicable protections include race, color, ancestry, national origin, religion, sex, pregnancy, gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, age, disability, medical condition, genetic information, marital status, and military or veteran status, as well as other characteristics protected by law.

You may request a reasonable accommodation or assistance with our process at careers@livework.inc. We will consider requests individually under applicable law. Please avoid including unnecessary sensitive information, photographs, government identifiers, or medical records in general submissions. If information is needed for an accommodation or another lawful purpose, we will explain the appropriate process.

Offers, verification, and independent choices

Clients make hiring decisions and issue employment offers. Verify the employer’s identity and review the proposed compensation, duties, location, remote or hybrid expectations, and employment conditions before accepting. LIVEWORK does not promise that a client’s statements or future employment conditions will remain unchanged.

Background reports, reference checks, and work authorization checks require the applicable notices, permissions, and procedures. Our general candidate terms are not a background-check authorization. LIVEWORK will conduct such checks only under a separately agreed scope and the legally required process; otherwise the hiring company is responsible for its checks.

You remain free to pursue other opportunities, use other recruiters, communicate directly with employers, negotiate, and decline or accept an offer. Please keep us informed when an opportunity we introduced progresses so we can coordinate accurately. We have no authority to accept employment or bind you to employment terms.

Confidential information

Use genuinely confidential client information we expressly identify only to evaluate the relevant opportunity. This obligation excludes information that is public, already lawfully known to you, independently developed, or lawfully obtained without a confidentiality duty.

Nothing restricts lawful discussion of wages or working conditions, consultation with professional advisers, reporting suspected misconduct, cooperation with authorities, or any other protected activity. These Terms do not create a noncompete or restrict your ability to work.

Ending participation and handling records

You may withdraw from a search or stop future recruiting outreach by emailing careers@livework.inc. We may end services for a legitimate, nondiscriminatory reason, including material misrepresentation or misuse of confidential information. Ending services does not create a fee or penalty for you.

You may request access, correction, or deletion at privacy@livework.inc. We will honor requests subject to applicable law, including required employment and referral record retention. Information already lawfully disclosed may remain with a client under its own legal duties and privacy notice; we will assist with forwarding requests when appropriate. Stopping presentation is separate from deleting records we must retain.

Legal framework and contact

For direct-hire services, LIVEWORK is a recruiting intermediary and is not your employer or employer of record. We are paid by clients and do not act as your legal adviser or have authority to bind you. No confidentiality or other provision limits nonwaivable rights.

California law governs, subject to mandatory applicable protections. Disputes may be brought in the California state courts in Orange County or the United States District Court for the Central District of California, as jurisdiction permits, unless applicable law provides otherwise. You may communicate with enforcement agencies without notifying LIVEWORK.
