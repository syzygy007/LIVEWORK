How it works

What gets built.

GearNew gear table: person, kind, make and model, Mac or Windows, serial (the UID), asset tag LW-0001 up, bought, cost, handed over, returned. Gear column on People, a Gear screen for the full list. Admin only
Day one sheetPrints from the Gear sheet with her name, email, serial and tag filled in, Mac or Windows steps. She sets her own password. The desk never stores one
Handoff formGoes out through Papers like the other employee docs, she signs, the green dot lands on her Gear row
PhoneHer own phone. The desk installs from Safari as an app, nothing to buy or manage
The Cardcard.html on livework.inc, same staff sign in. Front is name and the LIVEWORK mark, the code carries title, cell, work email, livework.inc
Card scannerSame reader as deedable, on the LIVEWORK project. Each card matched to a client by company name or email domain, else saved as a new prospect. Duplicates by email skipped
ContactsNew screen, one book across every client and prospect. Source and who scanned it on every row. To phone opens the Add to Contacts page
